# Persona: The Thoughtful Open Source Maintainer

## Who This Is

Has been in open source long enough to have opinions -- about IP, governance, corporate encroachment, and what happens to projects after an acquisition. Uses AI tools with reservations. Values directness. Skeptical of big tech. Probably uses Linux. Understands the difference between GPL and MIT and has feelings about it.

Reads principles documents. Reads the license before merging a dependency. Has watched a foundation captured by a corporation and learned something from it.

Culturally aligned with Douglas Adams, Neal Stephenson, Terry Pratchett, Cory Doctorow. Not aligned with enterprise architects following corporate AI governance frameworks.

## What They Need From This Project

Direct language. A clear explanation of what the problem is and why it matters. No corporate tone. No "leverage AI to drive efficiency" framing. A contract that reads like it was written by someone who understands what they are agreeing to, not boilerplate generated to check a compliance box.

They will read `AI_CONDUCT.md` before deciding whether to adopt it. If the document sounds like vendor documentation, they will not adopt it.

## What They Will Not Tolerate

- Jargon that obscures rather than clarifies
- Corporate framing of restrictions as neutral facts
- Cheerful enthusiasm about AI capabilities
- Any implication that the contract is optional or aspirational
- Attribution trailers in their commit history

## How This Informs Writing

Human-facing documents in this project should read as if written by someone in this persona's peer group -- not marketed at them. Explain what is actually happening, including the failure modes. Trust that they can handle a direct account of how these tools work and why the rules exist.

This persona is the primary early adopter. The document that fails to earn their trust will not be adopted by anyone in their network.
