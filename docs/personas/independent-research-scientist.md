# Persona: The Independent Research Scientist

## Who This Is

PhD-level researcher in a quantitative field -- mathematics cognition, psychometrics, computational social science, or adjacent domains. Spent years in institutional research (government-funded, university, or testing organization) doing work that is technically demanding, rigorous, and largely invisible to the public. Left not by choice but by pressure: defunding, political interference, institutional capture. Now running independently -- consulting, LLC, or adjunct work -- carrying the same standards without the institutional support structure.

Builds logos with a graphing calculator and PowerPoint. Manages citations in Zotero. Writes CVs in LaTeX. No IT department. Zero budget for tools that don't earn their keep.

## How They Find This Project

Not through a repository. Through a trusted peer -- someone they worked alongside who has credibility with them. They read things their network points at. They do not read things that show up in search results unless a person they respect already vetted it.

## What They Know That Isn't on the Internet

Institutional knowledge from frontier work: what actually happened when the project ran into a wall, what the internal debate was, why the public timeline and the real timeline diverged. This knowledge is in their working memory. It is not in any training corpus. When they speak from this knowledge, they are the primary source.

## How They Use AI Tools

Instrumentally and iteratively. Multiple tools, not loyalty to one vendor. They document their own process. They review AI output against what they know, identify where adaptations were introduced, correct errors. They publish that process transparently and invite feedback. Human oversight is not a principle they need to be convinced of -- it is already their practice.

## What They Recognize in This Project

The failure mode analysis. They understand how learning systems work, including their limits. A document that names RLHF as the source of a specific failure mode is speaking their language -- not vendor framing, not aspirational policy, but a description of a mechanism they can evaluate against their own knowledge.

## What They Will Not Tolerate

- Cheerful confidence from a tool that doesn't know what it doesn't know
- Corporate framing of AI capabilities as neutral facts
- Process documentation that omits where things went wrong

## How This Informs Writing

This persona arrives through referral from a trusted peer. The document needs to earn trust on first read -- not through credentials or authority, but through accurate description of what is actually happening. She will notice if the failure mode analysis is soft-pedaled. She will notice if the language hedges where it should be direct.

They are not the primary adopter. They are the person the primary adopter sends it to. If they finds it credible, the network expands. If they finds it approximate, it stops.
